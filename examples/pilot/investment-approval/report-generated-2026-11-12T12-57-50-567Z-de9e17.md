# test22

Northbridge Growth Fund is considering an EUR 8 million subscription for newly issued equity in Cedar Components under case NB-CED-2026-11. This report assesses the proposed valuation, the available investment authority and the evidence of compliance clearance using the committee minutes dated 3 November 2026, the transaction terms dated 4 November 2026 and the compliance file dated 5 November 2026. The proposal should return for an increased allocation decision and clarification of compliance status before it is treated as ready to proceed.

## R01 — Implied equity valuation

The proposed subscription implies a post-money equity valuation of EUR 40 million.

The fund would subscribe EUR 8 million for 20% of the total issued equity immediately after the subscription. Applying the valuation rule in the terms gives EUR 8 million ÷ 0.20 = EUR 40 million. Because the entire subscription purchases newly issued equity, the corresponding pre-money equity valuation is EUR 40 million − EUR 8 million = EUR 32 million. The terms state that the figures are exact and that no secondary purchase, fee, debt adjustment or other consideration affects the calculation. These are valuations implied by the proposed terms; the document does not record a completed investment. [Proposed subscription and valuation basis, S01](sources/transaction-terms.md#s01--transaction-terms)

## R02 — Investment allocation shortfall

The proposed EUR 8 million subscription exceeds the operative Investment Committee allocation by EUR 2 million.

The committee approved a maximum allocation of EUR 6 million after considering the EUR 8 million proposal. The limit is inclusive and applies to the full subscription, so EUR 8 million − EUR 6 million = EUR 2 million falls outside the approved allocation. The minutes expressly provide no waiver, increase or exception for the larger amount and require a new committee decision to increase the allocation. The proposed subscription therefore cannot be treated as authorized by the recorded allocation decision. [Operative committee allocation and requirement for an increase, S02](sources/investment-mandate.md#s02--approved-allocation) [Amount of the proposed subscription, S01](sources/transaction-terms.md#s01--transaction-terms)

An increased allocation decision is needed to support the proposal at EUR 8 million. Alternatively, a smaller subscription would require revised transaction terms: the supplied documents do not establish what ownership interest a revised EUR 6 million investment would purchase. The current valuation calculation remains based on the proposed EUR 8 million subscription for 20% ownership. [Approved allocation ceiling, S02](sources/investment-mandate.md#s02--approved-allocation) [Ownership and subscription terms, S01](sources/transaction-terms.md#s01--transaction-terms)

## R03 — Compliance clearance evidence

Compliance clearance is not established by the supplied file.

The acknowledgment dated 4 November 2026 confirms that Compliance received the submission; it expressly grants no approval and is not a clearance decision. No compliance decision appears in the supplied file. Because that file is not an exhaustive decision history, the omission does not establish whether clearance was granted, refused or remains pending elsewhere. [Receipt acknowledgment and limits of the decision record, S03](sources/compliance-file.md#s03--submission-and-decision-status)

The deal team should obtain the applicable compliance decision or confirmation of its current status before relying on clearance in its investment recommendation. This is a recommendation to resolve an evidence gap, rather than a finding that Compliance refused the investment or that no decision exists. Resolving that gap would not itself increase the committee's EUR 6 million allocation.
